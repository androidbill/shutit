How to Play

Everyone has a board of tiles numbered 1 to 12, all standing up.

Your turn: roll two dice (you always roll two). Then shut any tiles that add up to the total — a roll of 9 could shut 9, 4+5, 1+8, 2+3+4 and so on. Tap the tiles, then tap Shut. Play passes to the next player.

Stuck? If no combination of your standing tiles makes the roll, you're out for the round and get skipped from then on.

Round end: when every player is stuck, or the moment someone shuts every tile — Shut It!

Scoring: your score is the sum of the tiles still standing. Lowest wins. Shut the box and you score 0. With several rounds, scores add up.
